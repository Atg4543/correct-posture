    <p style="text-align: center; color: blue;">
        <b>Correct Your Posture While Using Computer</b>
    </p>

    <p style="text-align: justify;">
        You should maintain a proper sitting posture while using a computer. 
        Following are a few simple points that you can follow to improve your posture:
    </p>

    <ul>
        <li>Maintain a distance of at least 20 inches from the screen.</li>
        <li>Set the chair's height so your feet are flat on the ground.</li>
        <li>Sit straight on the chair.</li>
        <li>Look forward to avoid any kind of strain or pain in the neck.</li>
        <li>Level your eyes higher than the center of the screen.</li>
    </ul>
